# Spicanet

Independent product studio, operated by Egor Danilov from Koh Samui, Thailand.
We design, build and run our own software, research, travel and music products.
Every product carries an honest status label: live, beta or in development.

**Studio:** [spicanet.net](https://spicanet.net) · **Engineering services:** [spicanet.dev](https://spicanet.dev) · **Blog:** [spicanet.dev/blog](https://spicanet.dev/blog)

## Products

**Platforms and tools**
- [SpicaCMS](https://spicacms.com): SEO-first CMS runtime with production-ready template packs
- [SpicaSite](https://spicasite.com): managed websites and QR menus for small businesses (beta)
- [SpicaPlay](https://spicaplay.com): instant browser games with one player profile
- [ProxyLister](https://proxylister.com): manage, monitor and benchmark proxies from any provider (beta)
- [Protaura](https://protaura.com): a team of AI teammates for solo founders (beta)
- [Nomad Flow](https://nomadflow.money): privacy-first personal finance across countries and currencies
- [Zomia](https://zomia.io): voluntary microcommunities with rules, proposals and voting

**Research that publishes its method**

**Travel**
